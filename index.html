Privacy Policy — Block Empire: Neon City

Developer: Native (nativeuno.com)

Native ("we", "our", or "us") provides Block Empire: Neon City (Android package com.nativeinc.blockempire), a puzzle game with advertising and optional in-app purchases. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The game does not require an account. Your saved board, city upgrades, virtual items, check-in records, and language preference are stored on your device. The app disables Android cloud backup for this saved data.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & Google UMP)

Google AdMob provides banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads. Depending on the device and applicable choices, Google can receive Android identifiers, the IP address and approximate location it can indicate, interactions such as ad views, and performance diagnostics. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents advertising choices in supported regions. When privacy options are available for your region, you can change them in Settings > Ad Privacy & Consent (UMP). You can also manage your Android advertising ID in Android settings.

Learn more about how Google uses partner data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional digital purchases (such as Starter Pack, Remove Ads, VIP Pass, Piggy Bank, and Gem Packs) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your credit card numbers or billing credentials.

4. Product analytics

We use Amplitude to measure how the game is used and improve it. Events include installation, opening the app, session activity, and completing a stage. Amplitude receives a pseudonymous installation ID and basic context such as the app version, operating system version, and language. We do not send your name, email address, advertising ID, payment details, or saved board to Amplitude. Amplitude processes these events for Native in the United States. Amplitude privacy information

5. In-App Review & System Share

If you choose to rate the game or share your score using your device's system share sheet, those actions are handled directly by Google Play Services or the receiving app you select.

6. Children's Privacy

Block Empire: Neon City is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Deletion & Contact Us

Uninstalling the app removes its local save data from your device. It does not remove past analytics or billing records held off the device. You can contact Native to ask about your privacy rights. Because the game has no account, we may need additional information to associate a request with a specific installation. Google Play handles purchase records through your Google account.
