Privacy Policy — Block Empire: Neon City

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Developer: Native Inc (nativeuno.com)

Native Inc ("we", "our", or "us") built Block Empire: Neon City (package ID: com.nativeinc.blockempire) as a free-to-play ad-supported game with optional in-app purchases. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our game.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

We do not require account registration and do not operate a personal data backend server for this game. Your game progress—such as puzzle scores, Neon City building upgrades, virtual gold and gems, boosters, 7-day check-in progress, unlocked skins, and language preference—is stored locally on your device using platform storage.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & Google UMP)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open advertisements. Google and its partners may use your device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / IDFA), IP address, app interactions, and coarse diagnostic/crash metrics to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud.

Where required by law (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland under GDPR, and applicable US state privacy laws), we present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can update your privacy and ad-personalization choices at any time inside the app via Settings > Privacy & Ad Consent (UMP).

Learn more about how Google uses partner data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional digital purchases (such as Starter Pack, Remove Ads, VIP Pass, Piggy Bank, and Gem Packs) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your credit card numbers or billing credentials.

4. In-App Review & System Share

If you choose to rate the game or share your score using your device's system share sheet, those actions are handled directly by Google Play Services or the receiving app you select.

5. Children's Privacy

Block Empire: Neon City is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Deletion & Contact Us

Because game saves are stored locally, uninstalling the app removes local game data from your device. For any privacy questions or requests, please contact us at:

Website: https://nativeuno.com
